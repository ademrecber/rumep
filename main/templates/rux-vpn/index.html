On your new device, go to the Premium screen → tap "Migration" → enter your original TXID and your Recovery Code. Our backend will instantly transfer your premium tunnel to the new device.

Each premium subscription allows exactly one (1) concurrent IP connection. Connecting from a second device while the first session is active will automatically disconnect the prior session.
