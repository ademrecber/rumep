Privacy Policy

Last Modified: May 2026

RUX VPN has the strongest observance of data protection and uncompromising protection of user privacy. When using RUX VPN, we have absolutely no knowledge about your online traffic — browsing history, traffic destination, data content, or search preferences are NEVER monitored, recorded, logged, or stored. We also do not store connection logs, meaning no logs tied to your real IP address, connection timestamp, or session duration.

1. What Does This Policy Cover?

This Privacy Policy applies to all Services, proxy interfaces, routing nodes, and applications under the RUX VPN brand, and to the website rumep.net. By using our Services, you accept the practices described here.

2. What Data Do We Collect?

Our products support fully anonymous use. No registration, email, name, phone number, or password is required. We do not record any Personal Information (PII) during your use of the VPN tunnel.

A. Anonymous Information ("Non-personal Data")

We collect limited non-personal, statistical information solely to maintain connection quality:

  • Device model, operating system version, and general system properties
  • Preferred language and general locale
  • Date and time of connection triggers (aggregated, not bound to IPs)
  • Connectivity types (WLAN, Cellular) and mobile service provider names
  • Google Advertising ID (for aggregate diagnostics only — resettable anytime)

Non-personal Data is never associated with your Personal Information and does not permit identification of individual persons. It never includes your browsing history, web content, destination IPs, or DNS queries.

3. Strict No-Logs Disclosure

Through our strict No-Logs Policy, we guarantee:

  • We DO NOT know which user accessed any website, protocol, or internet service
  • We DO NOT know which user was connected at any given time or which node IP they used
  • We DO NOT store any database of original IP addresses
  • Your encrypted tunnel credentials (VLESS-Reality keys) are held in volatile RAM and deleted instantly upon disconnect

4. Blockchain Transactions

Premium features are unlocked via decentralized blockchain payments (USDT on BSC). In this process:

  • We store your on-chain transaction hashes (TXIDs) to verify premium duration
  • Your TXID is matched solely to your offline-generated 6-character Recovery Code
  • Transaction data, wallet addresses, and crypto history are completely isolated from your VPN browsing history

5. Children's Privacy

You must be at least 16 years of age to use our Service. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If we discover such data was collected, we will delete it immediately.

6. GDPR, CCPA & International Compliance

Because RUX VPN does not collect Personal Data, email addresses, names, or real-world identifiers, traditional data requests (Access, Portability, Erasure under GDPR/CCPA) are inherently fulfilled — there is no personal data database to retrieve or delete.

7. Data Retention

We retain TXIDs and anonymous premium duration records as long as needed to provide active subscription services. Once expired, this data remains historically archived to prevent double-claiming, but remains completely decoupled from any user traffic metadata.

8. Policy Updates

Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective immediately upon publication. Your continued use of the Services constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

9. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us through the channels listed on rumep.net.
